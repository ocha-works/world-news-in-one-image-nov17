One image that sums up the world news of Tuesday 17 November 2026: a front-page style infographic with six panels — Ukraine's 1,300-drone barrage hitting the Crimea Bridge, the tanker attack in the Strait of Hormuz, oil, gold and stocks falling together, Trump at the ASEAN summit in Manila, the COP31 draft deal to exit fossil fuels, and four allies urging Israel to open Gaza's aid crossings — plus a ticker of the day's other stories. The picture is drawn in code with `generate_image.py` so every word stays crisp; the stories and their sources are listed in `SOURCES.md`. `main.ts` checks the image against the competition gate (PNG or JPEG, at most 1 MiB and 1024×1024) and copies it to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
